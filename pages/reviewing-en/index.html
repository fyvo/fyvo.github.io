Reviewing and Editing Activities

This page documents my duties as a reviewer / editor for scientific jounals and conferences.

Editorial responsabilities

I am currently Action Editor for the Transaction of the ACL (as of 2016). I am also a senior action editor for ACL-ARR and a member of the steering committee of IWSLT. In the past, I was a member of the editorial committee of the French TAL Journal (2005-2025); I also served as associate editor for the ACM Computing Surveys (2019-2023) and for Computer Speech and Language (2020-2023).
