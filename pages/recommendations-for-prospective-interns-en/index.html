Recommendations for prospective interns

  1. For administrative reasons, I never enroll students preparing a degree outside of France for short term periods. 6 months is a minimum.
  2. I never enroll student outside my area of expertise. If you do not know what they are, you probably should not apply.
  3. If rule 1 and 2 do not apply, please send me a CV, reference letters, and a preliminary research statement explaining why you would like to do an internship under my supervision, and the kind of research question you would like to explore
